## 1. What the app is

Scrub Playbook is a place to keep your own notes about how cases run: setup, trays, instruments, sutures, and nicknames. It is a memory aid for surgical staff.

- It is not the hospital's preference card, the chart, an order, or a policy. **The surgeon and the hospital's official preference card always win.** When a note disagrees with either, follow the surgeon and the official card.
- It does not give medical advice, make clinical decisions, or replace training, competency checks, counts, time-outs, or any hospital procedure.
- It does not contain instructions for use. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and the hospital's policies for every device and product.
- "Matched a case" means someone typed their name after a case. It is not verification by the hospital.
