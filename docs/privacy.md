## The short version

Scrub Playbook keeps your notes on your phone. We do not have an account for you, a copy of your book, or a way to see what you wrote. Do not put patient information in the app.

## What the app keeps, and where

Your book (surgeons, procedures, the board, confirmations, flags, suggestions, and recent activity) is stored in your browser on your phone. It is not sent to us. The full list is in `docs/data-flow.md`.

Your phone also keeps the last 10 versions of each card and the cards you deleted in the last 30 days, so a mistake can be undone. They stay on your phone and are not in what you share or in your backups.

The name you type when you confirm a card or flag one is stored with that card on your phone and goes into your backups. Names of people you add under "People in the room" are stored with that card and travel with it when you share it.

## What we receive

The app is a set of files on a web host ([Vercel, Inc.], or the hospital's own server if it hosts the app). When your phone loads or updates the app, the host records ordinary request information: your IP address, the time, the file requested, and your browser type. We use those logs only to keep the service running and secure, and they are kept for [period] under the host's practices.

We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or crash reporting. We do not sell or share personal information.

## When something leaves your phone

Only when you choose to:

- **Share a card, hand off a place, publish a unit book, hand over publishing, or send a suggestion.** You pick the recipient and the channel (Messages, email, AirDrop, a file). That channel's own terms apply. Confirmations and flags are removed from what you share, hand off, or publish.
- **Export a backup.** The file goes where you save it. It holds your whole book, including confirmations. If you publish a unit book, its signing key is locked with your passphrase or left out.
- **Dictate.** Your browser's speech recognition may send audio to Apple or Google to turn it into text. Your unit can turn dictation off.
- **Open a product or company link.** Your browser goes to the manufacturer's site. The app does not tell that site which page you came from. That site's privacy policy applies.

## Patient information

The app is not built for patient information and has no place for it. It checks text for patient names, dates of birth, record numbers, and similar details and asks you to take them out. That check can miss something. You are responsible for not entering patient information, under your employer's policies and the law.

## Your choices

- See everything the app holds by exporting a backup and opening the file.
- Delete a card, a surgeon, or a place in the app. A deleted card stays in Recently deleted for 30 days unless you remove it for good; deleting a place keeps nothing of it.
- Clear the earlier versions of a card.
- Delete everything by clearing this site's data in your browser settings or removing the app from your Home Screen.

Because we do not hold your data, we cannot retrieve, correct, or delete it for you. For request logs held by the host, contact [contact email].

## Children

The app is for working surgical staff and is not directed to children.
