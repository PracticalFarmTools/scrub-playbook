# Facility pilot

One hospital, one service line. The personal book on a phone is not this agreement.

## What the hospital is buying

Staff record how a case actually ran: procedure, gloves, sutures, trays, nicknames. The official preference card stays the card of record. Scrub Playbook stores a pointer to it. Once the notes disagree with that review, the educator gets a worklist and updates the hospital system.

## What is not in the product

- Patients, record numbers, dates of birth, photos, implant lots, or serials
- Medication doses
- Copied instructions for use or technique guides
- Prices, rebates, or custom-pack contracts
- A score of individual techs
- A shared facility code

## What has to be true before real cards

- The Supabase project is covered by a business associate agreement
- An educator attests that in the app, which unlocks card writes
- Staff are invited by a one-time token, then sign in
- Traveler access has an end date
- Closing the facility cuts off every membership

## What the director can read at the end

- Which official cards went stale or were contradicted after a review
- Which of today’s cases had a card
- Which blocks staff corrected
- That a traveler account expired on the contract date

Company names and product links open the manufacturer’s own page. Those pages are not stored here.
